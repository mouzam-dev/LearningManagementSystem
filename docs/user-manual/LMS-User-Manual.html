Getting started

The LMS is a web-based learning platform that brings students, teachers, organizations, and administrators onto one site. Anyone can browse the public catalogue; signing in unlocks the experience tailored to your role.

01.The landing page

When you visit the site, you land on the marketing homepage. It headlines the brand promise, a search bar, the number of courses currently published, and quick "Get started" and "Sign in" actions.

Getting started · The landing page

02.Browsing courses without an account

Scroll down to see the most popular courses, each with its category, teacher, enrolled count, and an Enroll now button. Clicking Enroll while signed out takes you to the registration form with a return link, so you finish the sign-up and land on that course's enrolment page.

Getting started · Browsing courses without an account

03.What you get

Further down the page, the value-prop section summarises the four pillars of the LMS: video-first lessons, quizzes and assessments, verifiable certificates, and tools to teach and run cohorts.

Getting started · What you get

04.Creating an account

Tap Register from the header or the hero. Fill in your name, email, password, and pick a role (Student or Teacher). After you submit, a verification email is sent and you're signed straight in.

Getting started · Creating an account

05.Signing back in

Already have an account? Click Sign in and enter your email + password. Forgot your password? The Forgot password link below the form starts an email-based reset.

Getting started · Signing back in

Chapter

For students

Students discover and enrol in courses, work through lessons, take quizzes and assignments, and collect certificates as they complete courses. Everything lives behind the Student dashboard.

01.Your dashboard

Signing in as a student lands you here. The dashboard surfaces your in-progress courses, recent announcements from your enrolled courses, and links to keep learning.

For students · Your dashboard

02.Browse the course catalog

Use the catalog to discover everything published on the platform. Filter by category, search by title or description, and tap Enroll now on any card to join the course in one click.

For students · Browse the course catalog

03.Inside a course

Opening an enrolled course shows the syllabus (modules and lessons), your overall progress, and the course's announcements and assessments. Tap any lesson to start it.

For students · Inside a course

04.Your certificates

Finishing a course awards a certificate. The Certificates page lists every certificate you've earned. From here you can preview it, download a PDF copy, or share the public verification link with anyone (an employer, for example) so they can confirm it's genuine.

For students · Your certificates

05.Notifications

The bell in the header (and the dedicated Notifications page) collects everything aimed at you: new announcements, grading results, enrolment confirmations. Unread items show a coral badge on the bell.

For students · Notifications

06.Your profile

Manage your name, email, profile picture, and password from the Profile page. Any role can use this page — the change-password flow is shared.

For students · Your profile

Chapter

For teachers

Teachers build and run their own courses end-to-end: drafting modules and lessons, attaching assessments, grading submissions, and tracking student progress.

01.Teacher dashboard

Your home base. At a glance: courses you teach, recent submissions waiting for grading, and your active cohorts.

For teachers · Teacher dashboard

02.My courses

Every course you teach (draft, published, or archived) is here. Click any card to open its builder; click New course to start a fresh one.

For teachers · My courses

03.Creating a new course

Give the course a title, description, category, optional max student count, and a thumbnail. Once saved, you can keep editing inside the course builder and publish whenever it's ready.

For teachers · Creating a new course

04.Course builder

This is the workshop. Add modules to structure the syllabus, then add lessons (video, text, or document) inside each module. The right-hand panel collects everything else: Students, Analytics, Edit details, Publish, Archive, Duplicate. The Assessments section below lets you attach quizzes or assignments to the course.

For teachers · Course builder

05.Grading inbox

Submissions from your courses' assessments land here. Click any submission to open the grader — score it against your rubric (if attached), leave feedback, and publish the grade back to the student.

For teachers · Grading inbox

06.Question bank

Build a reusable library of questions. When you create an assessment, you can import questions from any bank instead of rewriting them every time.

For teachers · Question bank

07.Rubrics

Define criteria and point ranges once, then attach the rubric to any assignment. The grading inbox uses the rubric to drive consistent, structured feedback.

For teachers · Rubrics

08.Your profile

Same profile page every role uses. Update your name, picture, bio, and password.

For teachers · Your profile

Chapter

For org admins

Organisation admins look after a single tenant: its branches, the teachers who deliver content there, and the courses scoped to the organisation.

01.Org admin dashboard

Top-line metrics for your organisation: branches, teachers, courses, and students.

For org admins · Org admin dashboard

02.Branches

Create branches to mirror your physical locations or business units. Teachers and courses can be attached to a specific branch.

For org admins · Branches

03.Teachers

Invite teachers into your organisation and assign them to branches. From here you can also revoke access if a teacher leaves.

For org admins · Teachers

04.Courses

Every course owned by your organisation. Open any course to inspect its modules, enrolments, and metadata. Use this view to keep your catalogue tidy.

For org admins · Courses

05.Your profile

Update your own admin profile and password.

For org admins · Your profile

Chapter

For super admins

Super admins operate the whole platform: every organisation, every user, role-permission mappings, audit logs, and reports.

01.Super admin dashboard

Platform-wide KPIs: organisations, users by role, courses, recent activity.

For super admins · Super admin dashboard

02.Organisations

Create new tenants, see who runs each one, and drill into an organisation to manage its branches, teachers, and courses.

For super admins · Organisations

03.All users

Search and filter every user on the platform. Filter by role (Students, Teachers, Org admins, Super admins) or status (Active, Suspended). Bulk-import users from CSV with the button top-right.

For super admins · All users

04.Roles & permissions

Toggle which permissions each role has. Changes apply platform-wide.

For super admins · Roles & permissions

05.All courses

Every course across every organisation. Useful for moderation and platform-wide analytics.

For super admins · All courses

06.Reports

Built-in reports cover enrolments, completion rates, engagement, and per-organisation breakdowns.

For super admins · Reports

07.Audit log

Every privileged action on the platform — who did what and when. Use this to investigate incidents or compliance questions.

For super admins · Audit log

08.Your profile

Manage your own super-admin account.

For super admins · Your profile
